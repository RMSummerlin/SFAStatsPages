2026 NFL pass rate over expected by team. Pass rate over expected is an offense's dropback rate minus the rate a typical offense would have had in the same situations (down, distance, field position, clock and score), in percentage points, so a positive number means the team calls passes more often than the situations call for. Sacks and scrambles count as pass calls. Defense is the same figure for the offenses a defense faced. Last 4 covers the team's last four games.
